## Rulings

### R1 — pre-session daily-loss trip (findings 1.2, 4.5): the halt covers the coming session
`rules._halt_instant`: the clock's `next_open` when it is after now — EXCEPT when the market is closed before a session of the same trading date (`not clock.is_open` and `context.next_open_date is not None` and `context.next_open_date == context.market_date`) and the clock has a `next_close` after that open: then the halt lasts until `next_close`, so a recovery by the opening bell does not reopen trading that day. Otherwise unchanged (an intraday or after-hours trip halts to the next open; no usable next open → `now + halt_fallback_hours`). `aegis/policy/context.py` fills `next_open_date` (the date of `clock.next_open` in the same zone as `market_date`; None without one). Update the rule's docstring and detail. Tests: a trip at 08:00 New York (clock closed, next open 09:30 the same date, next close 16:00) → `halt_until == next_close`; the same without `next_open_date`, or with `next_close` unknown or not after the open → `next_open`; an after-hours trip (next open on a later date) → `next_open`; an intraday trip (clock open) → `next_open`; end to end on a tmp store: trip pre-session, then a fresh context at 09:31 with recovered P&L → REJECT by `halted`.
